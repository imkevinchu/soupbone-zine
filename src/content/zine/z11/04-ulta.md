We gave ourselves thirty minutes exactly. As soon as the caffeine hit my veins, everything became of the utmost importance and seriousness.

**SNIF**

*Spray Tan* - Not horrible. Sunscreen. 

*Coco Shimmy* - “Boring pineapple” (Eve). 

*Swede Tooth* - Smells uncannily like the monster we just drank. 

*Crumb Couture* - Bath & Body Works candle. 

*Naughty Nonna* - Don’t hate! old sweater, spicy. 

*Soda Snob* - Bitters/alc. 

**ELLA LANGLEY**

*Be Her* - Eve says this is guilty pleasure, but the sandalwood smells really artificial to me. 

**ELLIS**

*Vanilla Salt* - Smells expired. 

**BRIDGERTON**

*Dearest Tempting Peach* - Not bad! Reminds me of when I tried my mom’s Tocca perfume as a child. 

*Dearest Rebel Vanilla* - Not vanilla, but really good! 

*Dearest Graceful Hydrangea* -  Like a raincloud.

**BILLIE EILISH**

*Eilish* -  “The boobs are distracting” (Eve). “This smells like a girl you have a week-long crush on and then she moves away” (Eve). 

*Eilish No. 2* -  Super smoky, not great.

*Your Turn No. 2* -  Cat piss, if the cat had diabetes.

**SABRINA CARPENTER**

*Sweet Tooth Lemon Pie* -  I love Sabrina Carpenter and will tolerate no slander of this perfume, which is a photorealistic lemon pie/whipped cream portrait. I would not wear it, but I like it a lot. 

**KYLIE JENNER**

*Cosmic* -  Refreshing, inoffensive. Giving *eau de beige*. 

**ARIANA GRANDE**

*Cloud* -  Plastic. “It's like they forgot the smell” (Eve).

*Cloud Pink* - Plastic…marshmallow. 

*Love Notes Pink Woods* - Harsh. 

*REM Cherry Eclipse* - Plastic. 

*Mod Vanilla* - “Am I spraying too much?” (Eve). We determined that she was. 

*Mod Blush* - Play-doh. 

*Sweet Like Candy* - Pink lemonade. 

*Thank U, Next* - Someone ate play-doh and then threw up. 

*Ari* - Someone drank pink lemonade and then threw up. Very middle school. 

**DOLCE & GABBANA**

We started really vibing in this section. 

*Dolce Magnolia* - Classy! 

*Light Blue Capri in Love* -  Like a beautiful man dipping into a pool.

*The Only One* -  Sexy bubblegum.

*The One* -  Very jasmine, vaguely pleasant.

*Light Blue* -  I loved it! Blue lemonade.

*My Devotion* -  “Sweet miso soup” (Eve). Pleasing. 

*Devotion* - “Butterbeer” (Eve). 

*Devotion intense* - “Like a Cuban man. Like my grandfather at the peak of his life. He collected art forgeries” (Eve). 

*Dolce Violet* -  A jungle full of towering women. “You think they're trees and they're actually women” (Eve). 

*Dolce Garden* - Floral, powdery. 

*Dolce* - Baby powder. 

*Devotion for Men* - Expired peppercorns. 

*Light Blue Pour Homme* -  Tragically, this didn't preserve any of the things I liked about *Light Blue*. It smelled like spicy sour baby powder.
